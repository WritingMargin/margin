## Give the conversation room

An audio article can start with an introduction, move into the recording, and continue with your own notes. Readers can listen while they move through the page, or return to the written sections later.

Use the space beneath an episode for a transcript, links mentioned in the conversation, or a reflection on an idea that stayed with you. The text here is sample editorial copy, not a transcript or summary of the episode above.

> A good listening page gives the recording context—and gives the reader a reason to stay.
