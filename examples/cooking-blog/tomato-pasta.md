A skillet of softened tomatoes, garlic, and olive oil becomes a sauce with almost no work. The small trick is at the end: finish the pasta in the pan with a splash of its cooking water.

**Serves 2 · Prep 10 minutes · Cook about 15 minutes**

Want a little company at the stove? Open the [interactive pasta cook-along](/post/pasta-cook-along) for serving-size options, one step at a time, and help when the sauce needs adjusting.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | For 2 people |
| --- | --- |
| Dried spaghetti or short pasta | 200 g |
| Cherry tomatoes, halved | 400 g |
| Extra-virgin olive oil | 2 tbsp |
| Garlic, thinly sliced | 2 cloves |
| Chilli flakes | A small pinch, optional |
| Fresh basil | A small handful |
| Finely grated Parmesan | 25 g, optional |
| Salt and black pepper | To taste |
| Reserved pasta water | About 150 ml; use as needed |

Choose a wide frying pan, a pot for the pasta, a wooden spoon, and a mug for the cooking water.

## Make the sauce

Warm the oil in the frying pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and chilli flakes. Stir for 30–60 seconds, just until fragrant; keep the garlic pale.

Add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt. Raise the heat to medium and cook for 8–12 minutes, stirring occasionally. Press a few tomatoes with the spoon once they soften. You want a juicy, slightly chunky sauce. If the pan dries out before the tomatoes soften, add a tablespoon or two of water.

## Cook the pasta

Bring a pot of water to the boil, salt it, and add the pasta. Stir, then cook according to the packet, starting to taste a piece a minute before the suggested time.

**Before draining, scoop out a mug of cooking water.** Drain when the pasta is nearly ready, with a little firmness remaining in the centre.

## Bring it together

Add the pasta to the tomato pan with 2–3 tablespoons of the reserved water. Toss over medium heat for 1–2 minutes. The sauce should cling to the pasta without leaving a dry pan or a watery puddle.

Turn off the heat. Tear in the basil, add the optional Parmesan, and taste. Adjust the salt and pepper, loosen with a little more water if needed, and serve immediately.

[Callout tone="tip"]
**Watch the sauce, not just the clock.** Tomato size, ripeness, pan width, and heat all affect cooking time. Soft tomatoes and a glossy coating are your cues.
[/Callout]

## Make it yours

[Tabs title="Choose your pasta finish" variant="pills" accent="#42543b"]
[Tab title="Classic"]
Finish with Parmesan, torn basil, and black pepper. Add the cheese off the heat so it blends into the sauce.
[/Tab]
[Tab title="Plant-based"]
Skip the Parmesan. Toast 2 tablespoons of breadcrumbs in a teaspoon of olive oil until golden and sprinkle them on just before eating. Check the pasta ingredients if avoiding eggs.
[/Tab]
[Tab title="A little more substantial"]
Add 150 g of drained, cooked white beans to the tomatoes for the final 3–4 minutes. Add an extra splash of water if the sauce gets thick.
[/Tab]
[/Tabs]
