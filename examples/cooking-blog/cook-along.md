## Cook with me

Choose each step when you are ready. You can move backwards at any time; the tabs do not run a timer or save your progress.

[Tabs title="Pasta cooking steps" orientation="vertical" variant="boxed" accent="#42543b"]
[Tab title="1. Get ready"]
### Give yourself a calm start

Wash and halve the tomatoes. Peel and thinly slice the garlic. Put the basil and optional grated cheese within reach. Fill a pot with water and bring it to the boil.

Set a mug beside the hob to remind yourself to save cooking water later.

**Ready to move on?** The ingredients are prepared and the water is heating.
[/Tab]
[Tab title="2. Build the sauce"]
### Let the tomatoes soften

Warm the oil over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and optional chilli for 30–60 seconds, keeping the garlic pale. Add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt, then cook over medium heat for 8–12 minutes.

Stir occasionally and press some softened tomatoes with a spoon. Add a splash of water if the pan looks dry.

**Ready to move on?** The tomatoes are soft and juicy, with a few pieces still holding their shape.
[/Tab]
[Tab title="3. Cook the pasta"]
### Taste a piece before draining

Salt the boiling water, add the pasta, and stir. Follow the packet timing, tasting a piece a minute before the suggested finish.

Scoop out your mug of pasta water, then drain the pasta when it is nearly cooked but still a little firm.

**Ready to move on?** Pasta is drained and the cooking water is saved.
[/Tab]
[Tab title="4. Toss & taste"]
### Make the sauce cling

Add the pasta and a small splash of cooking water to the tomato pan. Toss over medium heat for 1–2 minutes. Add more water a tablespoon at a time if needed.

Turn off the heat. Fold in the basil and optional Parmesan. Taste, adjust the seasoning, and serve.

**You are done when:** the sauce coats the pasta, the pasta is cooked to your liking, and you want another bite.
[/Tab]
[/Tabs]

## Fix a small wobble

[Tabs title="Pasta troubleshooting" variant="pills" accent="#42543b"]
[Tab title="Too dry"]
Add a tablespoon of reserved pasta water and toss. Repeat a little at a time until the sauce loosens. If you forgot to save it, use a little hot water.
[/Tab]
[Tab title="Too watery"]
Keep tossing over medium heat for another minute. Let some liquid evaporate before deciding whether to add more. Pasta continues to absorb liquid as it stands.
[/Tab]
[Tab title="Needs flavour"]
Taste first. Try a small pinch of salt, a little black pepper, or more torn basil. Change one thing, toss, and taste again.
[/Tab]
[/Tabs]
