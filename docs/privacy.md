# Analytics privacy wording

Every Margin publication has a public `/privacy` page linked from its footer. That page describes Margin's built-in reader features. Each publication owner controls their own hosting, server or proxy logs, embeds, and optional services, so they should add a complete notice for those choices. This wording describes Margin's built-in readership analytics and can be adapted for that notice:

> We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages readers visit and how they engage with them. When country lookup is enabled, we use your IP address briefly to find your country, then record the country, not your IP address. Without country lookup, we record the location as unknown. Our analytics do not use tracking cookies and respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Analytics visits are cleared after 90 days when new traffic arrives.

Margin also uses request addresses temporarily for rate limiting and may send them to a configured CAPTCHA provider when a reader submits a protected form. Hosting providers, reverse proxies, and third-party embeds can have separate logging and privacy practices. Owners should describe those services and their own retention settings in their complete privacy policy.

For setup and measurement details, see [Analytics](analytics.md).
